========================================================
AEPLO JTAG ACCELERATOR FOR EMBEDDED PDLC AI OPTIMISATION
Speaker Notes - 4-Minute Presentation
~20 seconds per slide
========================================================

--------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 1 - COVER  (~16 sec)
--------------------------------------------------------
"Today we are presenting the AEPLO JTAG Accelerator for
Embedded PDLC AI Optimisation. The demo story follows our
NPI gates: PG1 Discovery, PG3 Development, and PG5 Validation
and Launch Preparation."

--------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 2 - THE PROBLEM  (~20 sec)
--------------------------------------------------------
"Embedded PDLC data is fragmented. Jira, Confluence, GitHub,
QA results, and JTAG or SWD hardware evidence are not connected
well enough for fast NPI decisions. That creates blind spots
from discovery all the way to launch readiness."

--------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 3 - SOLUTION  (~20 sec)
--------------------------------------------------------
"AEPLO becomes the connected platform. It treats the JTAG
Accelerator as a native capability, combines embedded hardware
evidence with PDLC traceability, and applies AI optimisation
through ROVO Studio, Copilot, and specialised agents."

--------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 4 - ARCHITECTURE  (~20 sec)
--------------------------------------------------------
"The architecture links Jira, Confluence, GitHub, Copilot,
ROVO Studio, Atlassian MCP and JTAG/SWD evidence. AEPLO turns
that connected data into orchestration, controlled fixes, gate
verdicts, and reusable launch evidence."

--------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 5 - CONNECTED PLATFORM STACK  (~22 sec)
--------------------------------------------------------
"This is the actual tool stack for the demo. Jira and Confluence
carry planning and knowledge. GitHub and Copilot support code
change. Atlassian MCP and AEPLO orchestrate work. ROVO Studio
is the AI experience layer. The JTAG Accelerator brings in
target MCU evidence."

--------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 6 - PG1 DISCOVERY  (~20 sec)
--------------------------------------------------------
"At PG1, AEPLO collects PRD context, Jira themes, Confluence
architecture, GitHub history, and board assumptions. AI maps
PDLC risk, trace gaps and embedded debug hotspots, then produces
a demo scope and validation plan."

--------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 7 - PG3 DEVELOPMENT  (~24 sec)
--------------------------------------------------------
"At PG3, the JTAG Accelerator attaches to the IDE, J-Link probe
and target MCU. It captures registers, stack frames and memory
evidence, routes diagnosis to specialist agents, and uses Copilot
to create a controlled GitHub PR linked back to Jira."

--------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 8 - PG5 VALIDATION GATEKEEPER  (~20 sec)
--------------------------------------------------------
"At PG5, AEPLO evaluates code, QA results, traceability and
hardware proof together. The gatekeeper produces a READY or
NOT_READY verdict with a prioritized action list for validation
and launch preparation."

--------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 9 - QA2RELEASE INTELLIGENCE  (~20 sec)
--------------------------------------------------------
"QA2Release closes the loop. Test failures and JTAG evidence
feed root-cause analysis, test optimisation and next-release
learning. The output becomes Jira actions, Confluence summaries
and improved gate evidence."

--------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 10 - DEMO FLOW  (~22 sec)
--------------------------------------------------------
"The live demo has three stages: PG1 discovery through ROVO
Studio, PG3 development using the JTAG Accelerator and Copilot,
and PG5 validation with AEPLO release gating and automated
launch-prep evidence."

--------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 11 - BUSINESS OUTCOMES  (~20 sec)
--------------------------------------------------------
"The value is practical for embedded teams: reduced defect
leakage, faster firmware root cause, more confident NPI gate
decisions, and AI-optimised PDLC work across planning, coding,
validation and launch preparation."

--------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 12 - CONCLUSION  (~16 sec)
--------------------------------------------------------
"The common idea is simple: connected embedded data plus
intelligent PDLC action. AEPLO, with the JTAG Accelerator inside
it, helps optimise every gate from PG1 to PG5. Thank you."
