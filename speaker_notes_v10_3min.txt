AI-DRIVEN EMBEDDED PRODUCT LIFECYCLE ORCHESTRATOR V10
3-minute speaker notes
Target duration: 180 seconds | 5 slides

--------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 1 - COVER (~18 sec)
--------------------------------------------------------
"Today we are showing AEPLO, the AI-driven embedded product lifecycle
orchestrator. The 3-minute story is PRD to firmware, firmware to JTAG
evidence, evidence to QA release readiness, and finally STQC submission
readiness for regulated IoT security and surveillance products."

--------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 2 - COMBINED FLOW (~62 sec)
--------------------------------------------------------
"This slide combines the rest of the lifecycle story. The problem and
architecture remain the same: embedded PDLC data is fragmented across
Confluence, Jira, GitHub, QA and hardware evidence, and AEPLO brings it into
one orchestration layer. From the Confluence PRD, guided by SDE Elements and
PSJIRA, epics and stories are created and listed for the user to pick. The
selected work creates a feature branch with PRD and QA context. At PG3, AEPLO
generates firmware from the datasheet and PRD, injecting SDK, BSP and OS or
RTOS context, then validates it against datasheet and acceptance criteria.
Finally, QA inputs, PRD criteria and JTAG evidence produce READY or NOT_READY;
NOT_READY becomes the priority list for the next release loop."

--------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 3 - JTAG DEBUG COPILOT (~32 sec)
--------------------------------------------------------
"This is the JTAG slide from V9 page 8. The agent starts from a bug or
story ticket, routes context to the JTAG orchestrator, connects to the
target MCU, and returns Git PR and Jira evidence. It diagnosed a
bootloader-to-application HardFault in 45 minutes, simulated sub-zero ADC
behavior at room temperature, and traced a boot failure to execution
redirecting to system ROM instead of internal flash."

--------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 4 - PG5 STQC AGENT (~43 sec)
--------------------------------------------------------
"The star of the show is the PG5 STQC agent. IoT security and surveillance
devices need BIS certification governed by STQC guidelines, for both
in-house designs and BTS sales. Earlier, developers spent months creating
evidence, validating code and preparing auditor submissions. The STQC
agent crawls the code, creates evidence, enforces hard rules like no
foreign characters, checks STQC ER guidelines, and decides whether the
submission pack is ready. If not, it highlights the section and creates
Jira tickets or stories for remediation."

--------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 5 - RESULTS AND CLOSE (~25 sec)
--------------------------------------------------------
"The realistic impact is material: STQC evidence preparation drops from
8 to 12 weeks into 1 to 3 days, PG3 firmware loops are 45 to 60 percent faster, JTAG root
cause is reduced to under two hours, and routed context plus prompt caching
cuts token use by about 35 to 45 percent. AEPLO keeps looping until QA says
READY."
